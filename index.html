More than a job title

Curiosity gave me range. Biomedical engineering gave it direction.

I work across equipment, quality systems and healthcare data because real engineering problems rarely stay inside one discipline. I learn quickly, document carefully and connect technical details with their clinical purpose.

How my range becomes useful

Flexibility means entering a new problem, learning quickly and connecting details that others may treat separately.

At the equipment

I look for readiness, alarms, sensors, power, calibration, documentation, and the clinical consequence of failure.

Inside the data

I build reproducible MATLAB workflows, question leakage and missingness, and report limitations as carefully as results.

Across quality systems

I connect technical work with ISO 13485, audits, CAPA, traceability, electrical safety, and responsible evidence.

Across people and countries

Experience in Egypt and Türkiye and communication across Arabic, English and Turkish strengthen how I adapt to new teams and environments.
